## Equipment
- p200
## Consumables
- Swab
- 10% (w:v) tissue homogenate
- 1X PBS
- 1.5mL centrifuge tube
## Protocol
- [ ] Add the swab to a 1.5mL centrifuge tube.
- [ ] Dilute the prion source 10-fold in 1X PBS.
- [ ] Add 50$\textmu$L of the diluent to the tip of the swab.
- [ ] Break off the handle of the swab and close the tube lid.
### Notes
- For extraction, see [[Environmental Swab RT-QuIC]] or [[Oral Swab RT-QuIC]]
