## Equipment
- 200$\textmu$L pipette

## Consumables
- Swab
- Tissue homogenate or enriched prion liquid
- 1X PBS
- 1.5mL centrifuge tube

## Protocol
- [ ] Add the swab to a 1.5mL centrifuge tube.
- [ ] Dilute the prion source 10-fold into 1X PBS (10$\textmu$L prion, 90$\textmu$L PBS or equivalent)
	- This ensures when you resuspend in 500$\textmu$L down the line, the pellet should ideally be at 10$^{-3}$.
- [ ] Add 50$\textmu$L of the diluent to the tip of the swab.
- [ ] Break off the handle of the swab so you are able to close the lid of the tube.
