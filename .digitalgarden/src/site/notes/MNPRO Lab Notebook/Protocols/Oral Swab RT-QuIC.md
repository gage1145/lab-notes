## Equipment
- BMG plate reader
- PCR hood
- Tabletop centrifuge
- Cup horn sonicator
- Biosafety cabinet
- -20$\textdegree$C or -80$\textdegree$C freezer
- Vortex
- p1000
- p200
- p10 or p20
- p2
## Consumables
- 1x PBS
- Sample swabs
- 1.5mL centrifuge tubes
- Sample Dilution Buffer
	- 1X PBS, 0.1% SDS, 1X N2
- RT-QuIC
	- 5X PBS
	- 2M NaCl
	- 100mM EDTA
	- 100mM thioflavin T (ThT)
	- HaPrP
	- Sample dilution buffer
		- 0.1% SDS
		- 1X PBS
	- 96-well plate
	- Plate tape

## Protocol
### Swab Extraction
1. Add 300$\textmu$L of 1X PBS to the tubes containing the swabs.
2. Vortex.
3. Sonicate in the cup horn
	- 3 cycles of 5sec on and 5sec off
	- Set to 50 for ~172🔽
4. Collect as much liquid as possible and add to a new tube.
5. Centrifuge at 21,100$\times$g for 30 min at 4$\textdegree$C.
6. Remove and discard the supernatant.
7. Store the pellet in the freezer or use immediately in RT-QuIC.
### RT-QuIC
#### Master Mix
11. In a 10mL tube add:
	- 2mL 5X PBS
	- 850$\textmu$L 2M NaCl
	- 100$\textmu$L EDTA
	- 100$\textmu$L ThT
12. Add the appropriate amount of HaPrP for the final concentration to be 0.1mg/mL.
13. Bring to 10mL with water.
#### Sample Dilutions
14. Resuspend swab pellets in 500$\textmu$L of sample dilution buffer.
	- This is considered a 10$^{-1}$ dilution.
15. Vortex vigorously.
#### Plate Setup
16. Pipet 98$\textmu$L of master mix into each well of the microplate.
17. Pipet 2$\textmu$L of the swab dilution to each well.
18. Seal the plate with plate tape before running.
### Typical Reaction Parameters
- Temp: 42$\textdegree$C
- Runtime: 48-72hr
- Shaking: 700rpm, 1min on, 1min off
